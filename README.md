# Discipline-Chan

A lightweight productivity timer with a small attitude problem.

Discipline-Chan tracks how you spend your time — and reacts accordingly.
Work consistently, and she approves.
Procrastinate too long… and she becomes visibly disappointed.

## 🚀 Overview

Discipline-Chan is a Chrome extension designed to:

- Track productive vs. non-productive browsing
- Encourage at least 1 hour of focused work per day
- Visually reflect your discipline level
- Make productivity slightly more entertaining

👉 Available for free, here:
[google chrome store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/discipline-chan/hhbcffjceaabhfohbgjibgnckkjipgpd?hl=no&utm_source=ext_sidebar)

## ⏱ Core Features

<img src="/resources/images/guide/all.png" align="right" height="500">

### 💤 Goofing Timer

Tracks time spent on non-approved websites.

### 💼 Working Timer

Tracks time spent on approved work sites.

### 🔥 Daily Streak

Work at least **1 hour per day** to maintain your streak.

### 📈 Weekly Graph

See how productive your current week has been.

### ⚙ Controls

- Pause both timers
- Reset goofing time
- Add or remove work sites

Time is automatically categorized based on your site list.

## 😠 The Anger System

<img src="/resources/images/guide/angry_maid.gif" align="right" height="300">

Discipline-Chan reacts primarily to your **Goofing Timer**.

- Small distractions → mild annoyance
- Extended procrastination → visible frustration
- Excessive time wasting → full disciplinary mode

Work for at least one hour and her anger resets.

Professional? Maybe.
Judgmental? Absolutely.
